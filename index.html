Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 27, 2025

AfterSnap does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your photos stay on your device.

Overview

AfterSnap ("we", "our", or "the app") is a before-and-after photo app built for iPhone. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use the app.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal information. We do not collect your name, email address, location, or any other identifying information.

Photos and camera access

AfterSnap requests access to your camera and photo library solely to allow you to take and view before-and-after photos. All photos are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to any server or shared with any third party.

Third-party sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. There are no analytics services, advertising networks, or data brokers involved in the operation of this app.

Children's privacy

AfterSnap does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
