Frequently Asked Questions

How do I take a before photo?

Open the app and tap the "Click!" button. You can take a new photo or choose one from your albums by clicking the blue icon in the bottom right corner.

How do I compare my before and after photos?

Open a album and click the blue Compare button. Select photos to compare

Are my photos stored anywhere?

No. All photos stay on your device. AfterSnap does not upload or share your photos anywhere.

The app is asking for camera/photo access — is that safe?

Yes. AfterSnap only uses camera and photo access to let you take and view your photos. Nothing is sent off your device.

I found a bug or something isn't working.

We're sorry about that! Please email us with a description of the issue and your iPhone model and iOS version, and we'll look into it.
